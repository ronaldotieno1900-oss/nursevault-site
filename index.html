Frequently asked questions

What format are the drug cards in?

Each deck comes as a print-ready PDF (double-sided, one drug per card) so you can print and cut them out, plus an editable PowerPoint version if you want to tweak or add your own cards.

Do you accept M-Pesa?

Yes — checkout supports both M-Pesa and card payments through Paystack. You'll get an M-Pesa prompt on your phone if you choose that option.

A class I need says "coming soon" — when will it be ready?

New classes are being added regularly. If there's one you need urgently, reach out and it'll help prioritize what gets built next.

Can I get all the drug classes at once?

The Complete Drug Reference Bundle will bundle every class together at a lower combined price than buying each separately, once all classes are finished.

Is this affiliated with the Nursing Council of Kenya?

No — NurseVault is an independent study-resource shop, not affiliated with NCK or any licensing body. It's built to complement your coursework and revision, not replace official exam prep.

A little clarity

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Everything you want to know before you get started. Still have a question? I’m here to help.

What does the free plan include?

A free account gets you started with NCK practice and sample questions, plus two care plans each month. Drug Cards are bought separately.

What comes with a paid subscription?

Weekly and monthly plans unlock every ready NCK Prep topic and unlimited Care Plan Builder plans for the duration of your plan.

Will my subscription renew automatically?

No. Paid access renews manually. Your access lasts for 7 or 30 days, and there is no automatic billing when the period ends.

What is Vault AI?

Vault AI is the built-in tutor for practice questions. When an explanation is not clear enough, tap “Still unclear? Ask Vault AI” to get a plain-language explanation of why an answer is right or wrong, then ask follow-up questions. Each account has a daily Vault AI allowance, and paid plans get a larger one. The written explanations that come with questions are free to read and do not use your allowance.

Are Drug Cards included in a subscription?

No. Drug Card packs are separate purchases, and the Drug Cards page shows which classes are ready or coming soon.

Can I use NurseVault on my phone?

Yes. NurseVault runs in your phone’s browser, so there is nothing to download from an app store. For one-tap access, add it to your home screen: in Chrome on Android choose Install app or Add to Home screen, and in Safari on iPhone tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. You need an internet connection to sign in and to use Vault AI.

Which courses is NurseVault for?

NCK Prep and the OSCE checklists are written for Kenyan nursing programmes, including KRCHN and BScN. Open NCK Prep to see which topics are ready for your level.

What is NCK Prep?

NCK Prep is the practice area for nursing students preparing for the Nursing Council of Kenya licensing exam. It has topic practice, past papers, timed mock exams and progress tracking. You can try free practice and sample questions with a free account, and paid plans unlock every ready topic.

What format are the drug cards in?

Each deck comes as a print-ready PDF (double-sided, one drug per card) so you can print and cut them out, plus an editable PowerPoint version if you want to tweak or add your own cards.

How can I pay?

Checkout supports M-Pesa, Airtel Money and card, and prices are in KES. If you choose mobile money, you will get a prompt on your phone to approve the payment.

A class I need says “coming soon”. When will it be ready?

New classes are being added regularly. If you need one urgently, message us on WhatsApp or email support@nursevault.co.ke and it will help decide what gets built next.

Can I get all the drug classes at once?

The Complete Drug Reference Bundle is planned for when all classes are finished. The Drug Cards page shows current availability.

Is NurseVault affiliated with the Nursing Council of Kenya?

No. NurseVault is an independent study platform and is not affiliated with, run by or endorsed by NCK or any licensing body. It is built to complement your coursework and revision, not replace official exam preparation.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version
  • We never sell your information and we do not show advertising.
  • We never see your card or M-Pesa details. Paystack handles payment.
  • Your camera is never recorded. In a mock exam it only shows you yourself, on your own screen.

1. Who we are

NurseVault is an online study platform for nursing students in Kenya. We are responsible for the personal information described here. We follow the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. You can reach us at support@nursevault.co.ke.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign up with a password, we store only a scrambled (hashed and salted) version of it, never the password itself. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name and email from Google, and no Google password.
  • Purchases and subscription: what you bought, the payment reference, and your plan and expiry date. We do not receive your card or M-Pesa details.
  • Study progress: your practice and mock attempts and scores, goals, exam dates, study plan, flashcard review schedule and OSCE checklist progress. We save this to your account so it follows you to a new phone or computer.
  • Messages and reports: what you send us when you contact support or report a question.
  • Technical and security information: basic logs such as sign-in and sign-up events, and your network address, used to keep the site secure and stop abuse and over-use.
  • AI feature text: if you use the OSCE coach, the messages you type to it.

3. Your camera in mock exams

The camera is optional. If you switch it on, your picture is shown to you in a small window on your own device. It is not recorded, stored or sent to us or anyone else. We do not use your microphone.

4. Why we use it

  • to create and secure your account, and let you log in;
  • to process your purchases, unlock what you paid for, and send receipts and download links;
  • to run the study tools (progress, readiness, plans and reminders);
  • to answer your messages and fix problems;
  • to keep the site safe, prevent fraud and enforce our terms;
  • to improve our questions and content, for example from the errors you report.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Emails we send

We always send emails you need, such as sign-up confirmation, receipts and password resets. Study nudges, exam-date reminders and subscription-expiry reminders are optional: you can switch them off in your account panel under "Email reminders".

6. Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with trusted companies that help us run NurseVault:

  • Paystack to take payments (card and M-Pesa);
  • Cloudflare to host the site, run our servers and store account and question data;
  • Resend to deliver emails;
  • Google if you choose Google sign-in, and to load the fonts used on our pages;
  • Anthropic (the maker of Claude) to power AI features. Only the text you type into an AI feature is sent. Your name and email are not.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our users and our rights. These companies may process data outside Kenya. We choose providers with appropriate safeguards, as the Data Protection Act expects.

7. What is stored in your browser

NurseVault stores a few things in your browser's local storage so the site remembers you: your cart, your login session, your display mode, and your study progress. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. How long we keep it

  • We keep your account and progress for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, we remove your account, your synced study progress and your sign-in sessions from our servers. Our sign-up and deletion activity log is kept for 90 days, and deleted accounts appear in it only with a masked email.
  • We may keep payment records for as long as the law and our accounting require.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you have the right to know what we hold about you, see it, correct it, ask us to delete it, object to how we use it and withdraw your consent.

  • Delete your account yourself: open your account panel and choose "Delete my account". This cannot be undone, and an active subscription ends immediately.
  • Anything else: email support@nursevault.co.ke with the subject "Privacy request" from the email address on your account. We may ask you to confirm it is you, and we will reply as soon as we reasonably can.
  • If you are not happy with our answer, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya).

10. Keeping your information safe

We protect your information with measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, limits on repeated login attempts and restricted admin access. No online service can promise perfect security, so please choose a strong, unique password and log out on shared devices.

11. Age

NurseVault is meant for people aged 18 or over. If you are younger, please use it only with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us information without permission, tell us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as NurseVault grows. We will change the "last updated" date above and, for important changes, tell you on the site or by email.
